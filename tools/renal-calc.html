Clinician tool (not for patients). Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

CrCl — Cockcroft–Gault mL/min

eGFR — CKD-EPI creatinine mL/min/1.73 m²

KHA CKD stage (eGFR 기준):

Albuminuria category A1–A3 needs uACR (not calculated here). Stage bands: Kidney Health Australia, Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management in Primary Care, 5th ed. 2024, “Detecting and diagnosing CKD”, p16. G5 = <15 or on dialysis; G1–G2 count as CKD only with other markers of kidney damage.
